Last updated 17 September 2026

Market Alert is a private publishing tool. It produces short daily videos and image posts about the US stock market in Hebrew, and publishes them to social accounts that we own and operate ourselves.

There is no sign-up, no customer account and no public service. The software is operated by its owner for one set of social accounts, and no third party can connect an account to it.

1 What the tool does

Market Alert reads a public Hebrew-language news channel about the US markets, composes a summary as images or a short video, and publishes it on a schedule to our own accounts on Instagram and TikTok. Everything it publishes is our own editorial output.

2 Who may use it

Only the owner and operator of the Market Alert accounts. The tool authorises against our own social accounts using credentials held by us. It offers no service to the public and accepts no user registrations.

3 Content and accuracy

  • Posts summarise publicly reported market news and are published for general information only.
  • Nothing published is investment advice, a recommendation, or an offer to buy or sell any security.
  • Where a post reports the outcome of an earlier post, the figures are computed from daily closing prices — the close before the original post and the close after it — and the method is stated on the post itself.
  • Market data may be delayed, incomplete or wrong. Decisions taken on the basis of it are the reader's own.
