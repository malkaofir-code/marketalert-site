Market Alert

A private publishing tool for one set of social accounts

Market Alert reads a public Hebrew-language news channel about the US markets, composes a short summary as images or video, and publishes it on a schedule to the accounts its owner operates.

There is no sign-up and no public service. The software has one operator and posts only to accounts that operator owns. This site exists to publish the policies that the platforms we post through require developers to make available.

Terms of Service What the tool does, who may use it, and the limits on what it publishes. Privacy Policy What is collected (nothing from third parties), what is held, and for how long.

· What it publishes

Short daily posts in Hebrew about the US stock market: what moved, why it is being reported that way, and — when an earlier post called something that later happened — the outcome, computed from daily closing prices and stated on the post itself.

· Not investment advice

Nothing published is investment advice, a recommendation, or an offer to buy or sell any security. Market data may be delayed, incomplete or wrong.
