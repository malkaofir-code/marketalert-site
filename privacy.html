Privacy Policy

Last updated 17 September 2026

Market Alert is a private publishing tool operated by its owner. It has no users other than the owner, and it collects no personal data from anybody.

No sign-up, no accounts, no tracking, no analytics on visitors, no advertising, and nothing sold or shared. If you are reading this because you saw the app named in a TikTok or Instagram permission screen: it is the owner's own posting tool, connected to the owner's own account.

1 What we collect

Nothing from third parties. The tool has no login, no forms and no visitor analytics. It does not read anyone's followers, messages, contacts or profile.

2 What we hold

  • Our own content. The posts we publish — text, images and video — and the public news items they were composed from.
  • Our own access tokens. Credentials authorising the tool to post to accounts we own, held encrypted in our own database and used for nothing else.
  • Our own post statistics. Reach, views and saves for our own posts, returned by the platform about our own account, used to decide what to publish next. These are aggregate counts about a post, never information about a person.
  • Daily closing prices for publicly traded instruments, from a public market-data source.

3 Where it is held

In a private database and object store operated by the owner, with access limited to the owner. Video and image files are uploaded to temporary public URLs only for the few seconds a platform needs to fetch them, and are deleted immediately afterwards.

4 Who it is shared with

Nobody. Data is not sold, rented, traded or handed to advertisers or data brokers. The only outbound transfers are the posts themselves, sent to the platforms we publish on, and requests for public market prices.

5 How long it is kept

Published posts and their statistics are kept while the account runs, because the record of what was published and how it performed is the point of the tool. Uploaded media files are deleted as soon as a post is live. Access tokens are deleted when a connection is revoked.

6 Your rights

Since the tool holds no personal data about anyone but its owner, there is nothing to request, correct or delete. If you believe otherwise, write to us and we will look into it and respond.

7 Children

The tool is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children.

8 Changes

This policy may be updated as the tool changes. The date at the top of this page is the date of the current version.

9 Contact

Privacy questions: message @marketalert.il on Instagram.
